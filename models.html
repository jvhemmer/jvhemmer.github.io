Laboratory components

Gas-tight mini spectroscopy cell

A compact 3D-printed cell designed for gas-tight spectroelectrochemical measurements using thin films (Ag in the picture) deposited on glass, quartz, ITO, etc. as working electrode. O-rings are used between the working electrode, and around the reference and counter electrodes to seal in gas and liquids.

Nanoparticle models

These models illustrate how crystal packing and particle geometry determine the facets exposed at a nanoparticle surface. The collection includes a nanosphere constructed from a simple-cubic lattice, a square-based nanopyramid, and 4 different sizes of truncated-octahedral nanoparticles.

Note how when increasing the size of the truncated-octahedral particles, the square {100} facets remain roughly the same size, while the proportion of {111} facets increase. Because catalytic activity is often strongly dependent on surface structure, this rational control of nanoparticle morphology and demonstrates how facet exposure can substantially improve reaction performance.
